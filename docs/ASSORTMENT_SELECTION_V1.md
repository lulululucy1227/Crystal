# Crystal Assortment Selection V1

Date: 2026-08-30

This is the first practical design assortment for the Crystal workbench. Supplier progression and purchase planning are paused. The purpose is to define **what the studio should carry/design with**, not where to buy it.

## 1. Core selection logic

Use three levels:

- **A_CORE** — high-frequency design materials that should appear prominently in the workbench and cover the six themes.
- **B_DESIGN_EXTENSION** — adds color, texture, rhythm or secondary design language without expanding the assortment too aggressively.
- **C_SIGNATURE_ONE_OF_ONE** — premium or highly distinctive focal materials intended for focal zones, one-of-one pieces or limited designs.

The workbench should prioritize **material role + form + optical quality + shape language**, rather than presenting a flat list of mineral names.

### B_DESIGN_EXTENSION

Blue Lace Agate, Kunzite, Bloodstone, White Phantom Quartz, Rose Quartz and Rainbow Obsidian.

These are not “fill the warehouse” materials. They exist to solve specific design needs: soft blue transition, pale pink/lilac light, earthy geological texture, internal landscape, restrained Sunrise color and optical black accents.

### C_SIGNATURE_ONE_OF_ONE

Larimar, Gold Rutilated Quartz, Black Rutilated Quartz, mixed Rutilated Quartz, Sugilite, plus collector/focal-grade Aquamarine.

These should normally be concentrated into a **small focal zone** rather than distributed evenly around a bracelet. Signature selection is determined more by the individual piece — pattern, transparency, inclusion structure, color and shape — than by species name alone.

## 3. Pearls and organic material

- **White freshwater pearl, 3–6mm rice/small baroque — A_CORE**: micro-cluster, light and transition; not a full pearl-bracelet language.
- **White South Sea Pearl, 8–12mm — C_SIGNATURE**: one or a few focal pieces for Glacier/Ocean.
- **Golden South Sea Pearl, 8–12mm — C_SIGNATURE**: Sunrise focal only.
- **Dark wood / agarwood-like structural spacer — B_DESIGN_EXTENSION**: thin irregular discs, short tubes or small segments; avoid large standard wooden rounds.

## 4. Hardware shortlist

The V1 hardware language is **925 sterling silver first**.

A_CORE:

1. 2–4mm silver micro spacer.
2. 20–30mm clean curved silver tube.
3. Thin silver frame/bezel for oval, freeform and polygon focal minerals.
4. Restrained silver half-cap / edge-cap.
5. Small geometric bar/block/ring connector.
6. 5–8mm small silver counterweight / transition sphere.

B_DESIGN_EXTENSION:

- Fine silver chain + minimal clasp for non-elastic constructions.
- Small abstract/botanical silver charm as a secondary narrative element.
- Oxidized/blackened silver micro accents for dark palettes.

Gold-tone hardware remains **reserve**, not a V1 core system.

## 5. Packaging shortlist

A_CORE:

- Compact matte white / cool off-white rigid presentation box.
- Cool-grey rigid drawer box for Signature products.
- Soft grey or pearl-white protective pouch.
- Material disclosure + care card.
- Plain protective branded shipping mailer.

B_DESIGN_EXTENSION:

- Cotton-linen envelope pouch in cool beige/grey.

C_SIGNATURE_ONE_OF_ONE:

- Minimal book-style magnetic box.
- One-of-One certificate card recording design code, composition, date/batch and visible uniqueness notes.

Packaging should remain quiet and editorial. Avoid default black-velvet/gold “luxury”, mystical graphics, chakra/healing copy, ritual language or unverifiable geological claims.

## 6. Shape language to stock for

The assortment should not be planned only by species. It must deliberately cover:

- 2–5mm micro bead
- 4–10mm round
- 6–18mm faceted irregular
- 6–14mm cube / rectangular block
- 10–25mm oval focal
- 10–22mm shield / polygon
- 12–30mm raw/freeform focal
- 2–6mm disc
- 6–20mm column/tube

A smaller species list with good form coverage is more useful than a large catalog of nearly identical round strands.

## 7. Do not prioritize in V1

- large quantities of generic identical 8mm round strands;
- rainbow/chakra mixed-color designs as a default language;
- large ornate zodiac/animal/ethnic charms;
- low-cost plated alloy findings presented as premium hardware;
- heavy black velvet + gold packaging as the default system;
- healing/energy/ritual product claims;
- buying many new species before core materials have enough shape/form/quality coverage.
